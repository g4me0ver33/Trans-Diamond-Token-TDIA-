# 💎 Trans Diamond Token (TDIA)

### Official Utility & Rewards Token of Diamond Bright AI

**Trans Diamond Token (TDIA)** is a blockchain-based utility and rewards token designed to power digital experiences within the [Diamond Bright AI](https://diamondbrightai.com) ecosystem.

TDIA connects blockchain technology with artificial intelligence, educational achievements, competitive experiences, and digital platform utilities.

---

## 🌐 About the Project

TDIA serves as the ecosystem utility and rewards token for **Diamond Bright AI**, a platform focused on artificial intelligence, interactive education, strategy development, and skill-based activities.

Our goal is to provide meaningful token utility through participation, education, and access to platform features.

## 🚀 Token Utility

### 🎓 Educational Achievement Rewards
Members can participate in educational quizzes and evaluations, earning eligible TDIA rewards for successful achievements.

### 🏆 Galaxy Poker Bot Competitions
TDIA supports rewards for eligible AI poker bot competitions and seasonal leaderboard achievements.

### 🔓 Platform Feature Access
TDIA is intended to support access to designated premium features, educational resources, and additional digital utilities.

### 🌌 Community Participation
The token is designed to support eligible community engagement and ecosystem participation.
